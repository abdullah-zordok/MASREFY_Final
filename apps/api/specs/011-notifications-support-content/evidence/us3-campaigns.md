# US3 — Templates and campaigns

Coverage verifies immutable template versions, bilingual variables, bounded
server-owned audience grammar, aggregate-only previews, optimistic lifecycle
versions, recent MFA/exact permissions, creator/approver separation, one-time
schedules, keyset batches, rates, replay dedupe, pause/resume/cancel, reconciliation,
and safe delivery status. Arbitrary SQL, URLs, expressions, recurring campaigns,
and expanded-user previews are absent.
